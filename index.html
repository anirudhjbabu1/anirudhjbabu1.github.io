— Work process

How I work

A structured engineering protocol that turns your technical brief into a tested, production-ready deployment.

i.

Discovery & spec

We audit your hardware constraints (SWaP), latency targets, and business logic to lock in a deterministic system architecture and milestone roadmap.

ii.

Simulation & design

Before touching fragile hardware or live capital, I validate kinematics, sensor fusion, or agentic workflows inside Gazebo SITL or sandboxed testbeds.

iii.

Edge & cloud build

Clean C++/Python nodes, neural networks quantized via TensorRT/ONNX, and APIs integrated with strict schema and safety guardrails.

iv.

Deployment & handoff

Containerized delivery (Docker/ROS2/Cloud), comprehensive documentation, live telemetry or web deployment, and long-term scaling support.
